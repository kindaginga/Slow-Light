Start from a preset, then adjust. Rotation is the strongest cue, flow the subtlest. If a result feels busy, lower flow first, then twinkle.

Star detection is tuned to the noise in your image's corners, so a clean, well-processed source gives better results than a noisy one. Very bright foreground stars will twinkle along with the galaxy.

Export uses your browser's video recorder. Chrome and Edge give the most reliable results at large sizes. If the export looks soft, the browser lowered the bitrate; a full-quality encoder is on the roadmap.
